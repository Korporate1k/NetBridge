NetBridge Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

NetBridge lets your iPhone or iPad share its connection as a local proxy, and lets your devices connect through a proxy server you choose. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: NetBridge does not collect, sell, use or share your data, and has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Traffic that passes through NetBridge

Traffic relayed by NetBridge goes directly between your devices and the destinations they connect to. It is never sent to the developer, and its contents are not recorded or inspected by any server we run. We do not operate any proxy or VPN servers.

When you use the Client feature, your device's traffic is routed to the SOCKS5 server you configure, such as your own phone running NetBridge. That server is operated by you or whoever you choose, not by us.

Information stored only on your device

  • Usage counters: how much data was relayed today and in total, used to show your usage and apply the free daily limit.
  • Connected devices: the local network addresses of devices that use your proxy, and any names you give them.
  • Settings and saved servers, including any proxy username and password, which are kept in the device's secure Keychain.
  • Free-trial start date, kept in the Keychain so the trial isn't reset by reinstalling.
  • Diagnostic logs, written only on your device. They leave it only if you choose to share or export them.

Deleting the app removes this information, except Keychain items, which iOS may keep until you erase the device.

Network requests the app makes itself

  • App configuration. NetBridge periodically downloads a small settings file from GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com). No personal information or identifiers are sent. GitHub receives your IP address as part of any web request, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Custom DNS (optional, off by default). If you turn on DNS-over-HTTPS, hostnames are looked up through the DNS server you set (Cloudflare by default), under that provider's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Local Network: so other devices can connect to the proxy on your network.
  • VPN configuration: used only by the Client feature, to route your traffic to the server you choose.
  • Camera: only to scan a NetBridge QR code. Images are processed on your device and never stored or sent.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on background keep-alive, to help NetBridge keep running in the background. Your location is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

NetBridge Pro (monthly subscription or lifetime purchase) is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. The app only receives confirmation of what you are entitled to and never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose any user data to third parties for any purpose, and we do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Children

NetBridge is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
